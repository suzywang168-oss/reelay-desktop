# Suzy Laboratory — Anime, Film & Product Design

Suzy Laboratory is the public portfolio of product designer and creative technologist Suzy Wang. It presents work across anime, AI filmmaking, content-production platforms, interaction design and creative technology.

## Live portfolio

https://suzy-laboratory.suzywang168.chatgpt.site

## Featured projects

- **Jetsen Production Partner** — a production-partner portal for script review, production collaboration, final delivery, distribution and settlement.
- **Jetsen Admin Portal** — a platform operations console for partner governance, finance, content operations, channel monitoring, analytics and RBAC.
- **Reelay** — an infinite-canvas AI filmmaking workspace for characters, scenes, shots, assets and generation workflows.

## Languages

The portfolio supports complete English, Chinese, Japanese and German experiences. English is the default language.

## Technology

Next.js, React, TypeScript, Cloudflare D1, responsive interaction design and public Sites hosting. Founder conversation requests are stored in D1 and prepared for Google Calendar follow-up.
